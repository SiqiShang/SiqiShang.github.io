Siqi Shang 尚思齐

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin, which I began in 2024. My research focuses on robotic manipulation and tactile sensing.

Previously, I received my Master's degree in computer science from Columbia University, where I worked in the Robotic Manipulation and Mobility (ROAM) Lab with Professor Matei Ciocarlie. Before that, I earned my Bachelor's degree in computer science at CUHK, where I worked closely with Professor Darwin Lau and Professor Zili Shao.
